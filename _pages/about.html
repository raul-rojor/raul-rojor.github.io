I am a mathematics student at UT Austin. I chose it because I enjoy the logical progressions in its theorems. When I first arrived, I planned to continue on a pure mathematics route; however, I couldn't help but notice the monumental impact that AI is having on so many fields. Thankfully, the logic behind machine learning's mechanisms is rooted in the same mathematics I came here for (linear algebra, probability, statistics), so the shift feels natural.

My interest narrowed once I started building a model and software myself. Emerging uses for multimodal models got me curious enough to train a small CLIP-style model, on a CPU, which taught me more about how contrastive learning works and what impacts its results. Being a student in a closely related discipline, I want to be a part of the force bringing a world that is responsible and helpful in its AI applications.

Two deployed projects in, I am now looking for problems that are faced by people on the ground. I hope to find them through internships, research, or other related projects where the results matter to someone other than me. My priority is to work where I can offer genuine help and learn in the process.
